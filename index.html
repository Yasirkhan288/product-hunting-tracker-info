Product Hunting Tracker

A private, owner-operated product-research tool. It helps its owner organize advertising research, review competitor and supplier evidence, and monitor approved products in a designated Google Sheets workbook.

How it operates

The application runs locally on the owner's Mac. With the owner's authorization, it reads and updates the configured Google Sheets workbook. A spreadsheet menu places requests in a queue for the local worker. Advertising information is collected through the official Meta Ad Library API where access is authorized.

The owner reviews product shortlisting, supplier matches and launch decisions manually. Advertising activity and research scores do not establish sales, profitability or product suitability.

This website

This is an informational website only. It does not provide access to the research tool, ask visitors to sign in, or display credentials or private research. The application is not offered as a public service.
