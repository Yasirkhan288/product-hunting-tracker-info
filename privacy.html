Privacy Policy

Effective date: 22 October 2026. This policy covers the private Product Hunting Tracker application and this informational website.

Google data accessed

The owner authorizes Google Sheets read/write access. The permission can technically cover the owner's spreadsheets; the application is configured to work only with a designated test workbook. It reads and updates product names, keywords, advertising observations, competitor records, supplier-review information, approvals, notes and related tracking data in that workbook.

Purpose and storage

Data is used to organize the owner's research and approved monitoring. Research history, backups and collection checkpoints are stored locally on the owner's Mac and in the designated Google Sheet. OAuth credentials and Meta credentials are stored locally, separately from these public website files. They are not published on this website.

Sharing and use

Google processes spreadsheet data and authorization requests as needed to provide its services. Meta receives the authorized advertising queries. The application does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to external AI services for training. Optional manual supplier searches are performed separately by the owner. No external AI matching provider is connected as part of this configuration.

The application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention and owner control

Historical records and backups are retained until the owner deletes them. Revoking Google authorization stops future authorized access but does not delete existing local backups or spreadsheet records. The owner can delete local research files and backups and remove spreadsheet data separately. The owner controls who can access the Mac and workbook.

Website hosting

These static pages use no application analytics, advertising scripts, sign-in forms or application cookies. When hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub may process technical request information, including visitor IP addresses, under its privacy statement.

Contact

The application is operated by its sole owner. For authorization questions, use the owner support contact configured in the Google OAuth consent screen. This site does not operate a public support or registration service.
